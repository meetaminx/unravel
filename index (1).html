    How answers are checked
    
    
    Four steps between your question and a cited line.
    
    
    A sentence is shown as fact only if it cites a passage, every number in it appears in that passage, and enough of its wording matches. Everything else is labeled as explanation.
    
    
    
      
    01
        
        
    Search
    Keyword ranking over every passage, with Zcash terms expanded (UA, ZEC, zats).
    
      
    02
        
        
    Select
    The top six passages go to the writer, each tagged with its source tier.
    
      
    03
        
        
    Draft
    Claude writes cited sentences and, separately, its own explanation.
    
      
    04
        
        
    Check
    Each sentence is matched to its passage. Any that fail move to explanation.
